# Booking Invoice Generator

Generate booking invoices with business details, guest/reservation info, amounts, and bank payment details.

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in a browser (double-click or drag into Chrome/Edge/Firefox).
2. Fill in the form: business info, invoice number/date, guest and reservation, amounts, and bank details.
3. **Preview Invoice** fills the invoice preview with your data (number of nights and balance due are calculated automatically).
4. **Print / Save as PDF** updates the preview and opens the print dialog—choose “Save as PDF” to download.

## Logo

The invoice uses **favicon2.jpeg** as the logo (browser tab icon and logo on the invoice). Place your logo file `favicon2.jpeg` in this folder.

## Files

- `index.html` – Form and invoice layout
- `styles.css` – Styling and print styles
- `app.js` – Form logic, auto-calculations, preview and print
- `favicon2.jpeg` – Your logo, used as favicon and on the invoice
